Frequently Asked Questions

How do I sign in to KaamConnect?

Enter your mobile number on the sign-in screen. We'll send a one-time password (OTP) via SMS — enter that code to access your account. No password to remember.

I didn't receive my OTP code. What should I do?

First, check that you entered your mobile number correctly, including the country code. If you still don't receive a code within a few minutes, please contact us at support@kaamconnect.app with your registered number so we can look into it.

How do I apply for a job?

Browse available jobs from the home screen, tap a listing to view full details, and tap "Apply" to submit your application directly to the employer.

How do I post a job as an employer?

Switch to the employer view, tap "Post a Job," and fill in the job details — including job type, salary, shift type, and whether accommodation or food is provided. Your listing will be visible to workers immediately.

What documents can I upload for verification?

You can upload a passport, Emirates ID, visa, employment reference letter, or skill certificate. Uploaded documents are automatically checked and typically verified within a short time.

Why was my document rejected?

Documents may be rejected if the file is unclear, does not match the selected document type, or appears to be expired. Please upload a clear, valid, unexpired copy and try again.

How do I withdraw a job application?

Go to your Applications list, select the job you applied for, and choose "Withdraw Application."

How do I delete my account or data?

Email us at support@kaamconnect.app from your registered number's associated contact, and we will process your deletion request.

I found a bug or have feedback.

We'd love to hear from you — email support@kaamconnect.app with a description of the issue (and a screenshot, if possible) and we'll follow up.

Still need help?

Reach out anytime at support@kaamconnect.app and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.
