Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your KaamConnect account and associated data at any time.

Option 1: Delete from within the app

If you're a Worker:

  1. Open the KaamConnect app and sign in
  2. Go to the Profile tab (bottom navigation)
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete My Account
  4. Confirm the request when prompted

If you're an Employer:

  1. Open the KaamConnect app and sign in
  2. On your home screen (Employer Dashboard), scroll down
  3. Tap Delete My Account
  4. Confirm the request when prompted

Your deletion request is submitted immediately once you confirm in the app.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you no longer have access to the app, email us from the number registered on your account:

Email: support@kaamconnect.app

Subject: Account Deletion Request — include your registered mobile number so we can locate your account.

What gets deleted

DataWhat happens
Profile information (name, skills, experience, languages, nationality, references)Deleted
Uploaded documents (passport, Emirates ID, visa, certificates)Deleted
Job postings (employer accounts)Deleted / deactivated
Messages sent to other usersDeleted from your account; may remain visible to the other party as part of their own conversation history
Application historyDeleted
Records required for legal, tax, or fraud-prevention purposesRetained only as long as legally required, then deleted

Timeline

Account and data deletion requests are processed within 14 days. You'll receive a confirmation once your account has been deleted.

Questions

If you have questions about this process, contact us at support@kaamconnect.app. See our Privacy Policy for more on how we handle your data.
