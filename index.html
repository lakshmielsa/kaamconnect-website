KaamConnect

Find work or hire workers across the UAE — fast, direct, and transparent.

Why KaamConnect

Finding reliable work — or a reliable worker — in the UAE shouldn't take weeks, agents, or hidden fees. KaamConnect connects job seekers and employers directly, with clear job details up front: pay, shift type, accommodation, and food provisions, all visible before anyone applies.

For Job Seekers

  • Browse verified job listings across all Emirates
  • Filter by language, shift type, and job duration
  • See accommodation and food benefits upfront
  • Apply directly, with no agent fees
  • Get notified instantly when new jobs match your skills

For Employers

  • Post job openings in minutes
  • Reach a ready-to-work pool of candidates across the UAE
  • Set clear requirements: shift type, pay, and benefits
  • Manage every application from one place
  • Message applicants directly through the app

Built for the UAE

KaamConnect is designed specifically around how hiring works in the UAE — supporting multiple languages, region-specific job categories, and the documentation (Emirates ID, visa, passport) that employers and workers already use every day.

Simple, Secure Sign-In

No passwords to remember. Sign in with your mobile number and a one-time code sent by SMS — that's it.
